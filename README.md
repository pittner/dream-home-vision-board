# Dream Home Vision Board 2027

36 home goals for 2027 in 6 groups (dream home, declutter and organise, renovate and fix, cozy and beautiful, garden and outdoors, home money and calm). Each goal has why it matters and a first step for this week, plus 12 questions and a 45-minute plan.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/dream-home-vision-board/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=home_2027) with the first step under it; save it as a 1080x1920 lock screen and see your home goal every day until it is done
- Or type your own home goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
