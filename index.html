12 questions before you choose your home goals

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to the goals that will actually stick.

  1. Which room in my home made me happiest in 2026, and why?
  2. Which room do I avoid or feel stressed in?
  3. What do I tolerate at home that I could fix in a weekend?
  4. Do I want to move in 2027, or make this place better?
  5. What does home need to give me: calm, space, light or people?
  6. Which unfinished project do I walk past every day?
  7. How much did my home cost me per month in 2026, all in?
  8. What would I get rid of if I moved tomorrow?
  9. Who do I want to welcome into my home more often?
  10. Which home chore causes the most tension, and with whom?
  11. What is one small change that would make every morning easier?
  12. If 2027 goes really well, what does my home look like by next December?

Plan your home year in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: walk through your home. Go room by room with your phone and note what gives you energy and what drains it.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below. Short, honest answers are enough; nobody else reads them.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three home goals. Not ten. One big goal, one small fix and one thing that makes home cozier.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your main home goal and its first step into a phone lock screen, so you see it every day until it is done.

Questions

What should go on a dream home vision board?

Put the home goal in your own words, a first step you can do this week, and your reason why. This page has 36 home goals in 6 groups (dream home, declutter and organise, renovate and fix, cozy and beautiful, garden and outdoors, home money and calm), each with a first step, so you can pick three and make them visible.

Does a home vision board actually work?

A board does not paint the walls by itself, but seeing the goal every day keeps it in front of you when you decide how to spend your weekends and money. The habit that works best is putting it on your phone lock screen.

How many home goals should I set for 2027?

Three is enough: one big goal, one small fix and one thing that makes home cozier. Fewer goals get done more often.

How do I save for my dream home?

Know the real number (deposit, fees, moving), divide it by the months left and save that amount automatically every payday, before you spend on anything else.
